    About

    I've designed for magazines, salsa makers, pastry shops, a coffee roaster, a patient app and a US marketing agency, usually from the seat between the creative work and the people who have to sell it.

    That middle seat taught me to explain design decisions in plain language and to defend the ones that matter. I've led small teams of two or three designers, and I write my own copy in Spanish and English.

    My production workflow folds in AI tools like Midjourney, Firefly and Claude where they save time, and leaves them out where they flatten the work. I wrote a 33-page guide on AI for marketing in Spanish, mostly to sort out my own thinking.
